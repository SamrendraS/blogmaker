[title]: <> (Clothing Choices and True Confidence: A Mother's Perspective)

What really bothers me is not the girls/ladies wearing shorts or half-naked clothes, but their body language to hide their nakedness by pulling their dresses down as if these will become long simply by pulling, or ladies trying to hide their parts with pallu or dupatta. All this shows that inside your brain you know that whatever you are wearing is unnecessarily putting you in trouble. Then why don't you want to live free, confident and strong?

We all wear clothes to look more elegant, confident and protected, otherwise God has brought all of us on this earth fully naked. But the clothes we put on protect us, make us look beautiful, safe and secured.

It is just a mindset that we being human, the super creation of God, blessed with thoughtful brain are tuned to protect ourselves from all kinds of danger, though this quality is inborn in everyone existing on this earth. But we being the super creature of God have more responsibility to save ourselves first.

So I feel it is the parents', especially mother's duty to guide her daughters to dress up elegantly. For this, the mothers themselves need to ponder over and remain disciplined and discourage the use of short clothes or torn jeans as your culture and sanskaar can be best taught by your family. In the market, people are befooling you and you are just letting them make you foolish. Don't simply imitate the others, use your own wit. Never compromise your dignity, your confidence, your protection, your independence with cheap actions.

I personally feel real pride, security, confidence, protection comes from within. If you are well dressed, your power will be more from your inner self. Just try it and see the difference. Less clothes don't make you attractive, in fact they make you vulnerable to danger which is very well known to your subconscious mind, that only guides you to pull your dress down and down.

Don't follow Bollywood actors. They have their security guards with them. They do all this to earn more money. They are not patriots. They are the true enemies of our country. Our elders never used to let us wear torn clothes, they either used to rafoo it, vulcanize it or throw it or use it as rags.

So my dear, do spend some time for yourself and keep yourself at priority, then decide with all your brain what is correct and take the needful action. You are the best judge of your life. You can correct your elders also if they are guiding you wrong. Awake. This is the time to analyse.
