[title]: <> (Civic Sense and Pet Ownership: A Morning Observation)

A very fine morning, everything is perfect. Sunrise is beautiful, birds are chirping. The sweeper is honestly sweeping the roads. Everything appears to be so good to be a resident of a beautiful city of India. My country is really progressing.

Just an hour later what do I see, to my shock a lady who seems to belong to a very good family as her husband is working in a reputed company, her children are also educated and earning on their own, means well settled, comes on the street with her dog, stands in the center of the road and watches her dog poo in the middle of a well-swept road. She makes no efforts to stop her dog. In fact, she waits for her to finish its work and after it is done, she coolly moves away with her dog very proudly.

My question is to the people who talked so loudly and created a big mess, when our Prime Minister Sh. Narendra Modi taught people to use toilets from Lal Kila, can't these people use their own brain and train their pets to use toilets at their home? When they can teach their pets everything to play with them, jump with them, entertain them in their ways. Do such people wait for Modi ji to guide them for this civic sense also? This is really a very big problem. I think we all need to ponder over it. Poo is poo, may it be from animals or people, contains all bacteria which is the cause for making so well-cleaned roads, wheels of cars, feet of passersby, messy and unhygienic.

This is the sense which needs to be developed within oneself because if you try to correct them, their reply is readymade - that these roads don't belong to you. This is not the problem of a single lady, it happens with every pet owner. They accept their pets to be theirs but roads of the country are not theirs. They don't even respect the efforts made by the poor sweeper or the government. 

So the simple solution I feel is that it must be made mandatory for every pet owner to train their dog and own a street dog and take care of their needs.
